## Design Context

### Users

AoS Reminders serves Age of Sigmar players who want dependable, phase-ordered reminders without
having to learn the application's data model. Internal accuracy-review tools are used by
rules-knowledgeable reviewers who may be unfamiliar with software, provenance, and certification
terminology. Reviewers may feel uncertain when asked to judge unfamiliar evidence, so the
interface must make the immediate question and next action obvious.

### Brand Personality

Familiar, trustworthy, and calm. The product should feel like the established AoS Reminders
community tool: direct, practical, and respectful of the user's attention.

### Aesthetic Direction

Preserve the live application's recognizable dark-blue masthead, teal accents, typography,
spacing, cards, and responsive behavior. Accuracy-review tools should feel like a focused reading
desk within that visual language, not a developer console or migration workbench. Use progressive
disclosure so source text and the decision are prominent while checksums, record IDs, and other
audit metadata remain available but secondary.

### Design Principles

- Lead with the single question the user must answer and explain why it matters in plain language.
- Use Age of Sigmar and everyday language; explain or hide pipeline terminology.
- Separate source reading from generated-value comparison so blind review remains trustworthy.
- Make uncertainty safe and precise: explain when to choose “I can't determine this from the
  source” without encouraging guessing.
- Preserve drafts, show progress, and make sealing consequences explicit before the irreversible
  step.
- Maintain accessible contrast, keyboard operation, visible focus, and complete mobile
  functionality.
